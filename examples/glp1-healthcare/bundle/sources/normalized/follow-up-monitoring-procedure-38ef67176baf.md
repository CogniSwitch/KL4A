# GLP-1 Follow-up Monitoring Procedure

## Follow-up Monitoring

Patients should receive follow-up contact within 14 days after GLP-1 therapy initiation.

Staff must record dose tolerance and adverse event symptoms during follow-up.

## Dose Titration

Clinicians must confirm patient tolerance before dose escalation.

Clinicians should defer dose escalation when severe gastrointestinal symptoms are reported.
