# Product Category Returns & Warranty Policy

## 1. Small Appliances

Small Appliances must be returned within 21 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Small Appliances must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 10% restocking fee. Small Appliances carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
