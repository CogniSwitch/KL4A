## 5. Prescription Eyewear

Prescription Eyewear must be returned within 7 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Prescription Eyewear must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 0% restocking fee. Prescription Eyewear carries a 12-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
