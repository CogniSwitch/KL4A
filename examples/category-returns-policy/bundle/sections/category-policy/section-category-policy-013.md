## 12. Tablet Devices

Tablet Devices must be returned within 21 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Tablet Devices must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 15% restocking fee. Tablet Devices carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
