# Customer Support & Returns Policy

This worked example shows a customer-support policy transformed into an SOP
Knowledge Bundle. It contains nine source sections, 18 knowledge pieces, their
supporting evidence and relations, decision rules, and task-ready contexts.

[Browse the complete bundle in the repository](https://github.com/CogniSwitch/KL4A/tree/main/examples/customer-support-policy).

To query a local checkout:

```bash
sopkb-cli knowledge search examples/customer-support-policy/bundle "restocking"
```
