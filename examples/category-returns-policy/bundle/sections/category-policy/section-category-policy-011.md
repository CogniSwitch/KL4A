## 10. Desktop Computers

Desktop Computers must be returned within 30 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Desktop Computers must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 15% restocking fee. Desktop Computers carry a 24-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
