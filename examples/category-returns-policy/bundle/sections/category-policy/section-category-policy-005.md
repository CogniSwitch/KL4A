## 4. Outdoor Furniture

Outdoor Furniture must be returned within 14 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Outdoor Furniture must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 25% restocking fee. Outdoor Furniture carries a 6-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
