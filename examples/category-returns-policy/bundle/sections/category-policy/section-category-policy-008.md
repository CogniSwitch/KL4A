## 7. Power Tools

Power Tools must be returned within 30 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Power Tools must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 15% restocking fee. Power Tools carry a 36-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
