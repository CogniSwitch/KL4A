# Customer Support & Returns Policy

## 1. General Return Window

Customers must initiate a return within 30 days of delivery to receive a full refund. Items returned between day 31 and day 45 must be processed as store credit only. Items returned after day 45 must not receive any refund or credit.

## 2. Condition Requirements

Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, and must include all accessories and manuals. Staff must apply a 15% restocking fee to items missing original packaging. Items showing signs of use must not be accepted for return under this policy.

## 3. Electronics Exceptions

Electronics must be returned within 14 days of delivery, regardless of the general return window described in Section 1. Opened software and digital license keys must be treated as final sale and must not be accepted for return under any circumstances.

## 4. Final Sale Items

Clearance items marked "Final Sale" at checkout must not be accepted for return or exchange. Custom or personalized items must be treated as final sale once production has begun.

## 5. Damaged or Defective Items

Customers must report damaged or defective items within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for a free replacement or full refund including return shipping costs. Reports made after 48 hours must be handled under the general return window described in Section 1, with return shipping paid by the customer.

## 6. Refund Processing

Staff must issue approved refunds to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the returned item being received and inspected. Store credit issued under Section 1 must not expire and may be combined with future promotional discounts.

## 7. Exchanges

Staff must waive the restocking fee described in Section 2 for exchanges of the same item in a different size or color, provided the request is made within the general return window described in Section 1. Exchanges for a different product entirely must be processed as a return followed by a new purchase.

## 8. Escalation and Manager Approval

Returns requested outside any window described above must receive manager approval before processing. Staff must record manager-approved exceptions in the order notes before a refund or credit is issued.
