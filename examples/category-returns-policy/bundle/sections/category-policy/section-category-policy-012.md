## 11. Mobile Phones

Mobile Phones must be returned within 14 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Mobile Phones must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 20% restocking fee. Mobile Phones carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
