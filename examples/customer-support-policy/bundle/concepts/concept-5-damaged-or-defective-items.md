# 5. Damaged or Defective Items

## Knowledge Pieces

- [Customers must report damaged or defective items within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for a free replacement or full refund including return shipping costs.](../knowledge/ki-support-policy-v1-000011.md)
- [Reports made after 48 hours must be handled under the general return window described in Section 1, with return shipping paid by the customer.](../knowledge/ki-support-policy-v1-000012.md)
