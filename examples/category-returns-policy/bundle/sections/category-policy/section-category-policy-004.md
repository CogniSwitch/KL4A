## 3. Indoor Furniture

Indoor Furniture must be returned within 30 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Indoor Furniture must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 15% restocking fee. Indoor Furniture carries a 12-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
