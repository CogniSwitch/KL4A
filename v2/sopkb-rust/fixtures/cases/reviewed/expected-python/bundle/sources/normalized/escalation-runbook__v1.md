# Escalation Runbook

## Initial Notification

On-call staff must confirm receipt of the alert within 15 minutes.

## Secondary Contact

Clinicians should route unresolved alerts to the secondary on-call physician.

## Vendor Escalation

The vendor liaison must record every escalation in the incident log.

## Legal Notification

Staff should confirm legal has been notified for any reportable event.

## Patient Communication

Care coordinators must confirm the patient has been informed of the outcome.

## Post-Incident Review

The safety committee should record findings within five business days.
