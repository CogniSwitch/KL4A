## 8. Hand Tools

Hand Tools must be returned within 60 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Hand Tools must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 5% restocking fee. Hand Tools carry a 60-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
