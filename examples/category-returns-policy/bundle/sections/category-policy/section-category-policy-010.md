## 9. Laptop Computers

Laptop Computers must be returned within 14 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Laptop Computers must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 15% restocking fee. Laptop Computers carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
