## 6. Non-Prescription Sunglasses

Non-Prescription Sunglasses must be returned within 30 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Non-Prescription Sunglasses must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 10% restocking fee. Non-Prescription Sunglasses carry a 24-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
