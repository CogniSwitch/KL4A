# Primary Care GLP-1 Prescribing SOP

## Intake Requirements

Clinicians must confirm patient identity before reviewing GLP-1 therapy eligibility.

Clinicians must document current medications, diabetes history, weight history, and relevant comorbidities.

## Contraindication Screening

Clinicians must screen for pregnancy, pancreatitis history, medullary thyroid carcinoma history, and multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2 history.

Patients with uncertain contraindication history should be routed for clinical review before therapy is prescribed.

## Follow-up Monitoring

Patients should receive follow-up contact within 30 days after GLP-1 therapy initiation.
