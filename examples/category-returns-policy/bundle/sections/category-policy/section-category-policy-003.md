## 2. Major Appliances

Major Appliances must be returned within 45 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. Major Appliances must include original packaging and all accessories to avoid a 20% restocking fee. Major Appliances carry a 24-month manufacturer warranty administered directly by the manufacturer.
